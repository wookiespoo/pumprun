PumpRun audio
-------------
SFX + music loops in this folder are original short WAV samples authored for
the game (soft envelopes, no speech). License: CC0 1.0 public domain.

To swap in Kenney / Pixabay / Freesound CC0 clips, overwrite:
  coin.wav          — soft gold-coin blip (pitch-rises on a streak)
  register.wav      — cash-register cha-ching (every 100 bags)
  vault.wav         — vault whoosh (every 10,000 bags)
  jump.wav          — jump whoosh
  land.wav          — landing tap
  crash.wav         — rugged hit
  power_magnet.wav  — magnet chime
  power_shield.wav  — shield chime
  step.wav          — foot tap
  music.wav         — synthwave / electro-hop bed (19.2s / 100 BPM, loops under SFX)
  music_perc.wav    — kick/hat/snare layer (fades in with distance)
  music_bass.wav    — sub pulse layer
  music_drive.wav   — shaker / clap / air layer
  slide.wav         — slide whoosh
  ramp.wav          — ramp-onto-bus swell
  whoosh.wav        — near-miss doppler
  catch.wav         — cop-catch sting
  stumble.wav       — cop hits an obstacle (thud / grunt)
  riser.wav         — speed / milestone riser

Layers are generated by tools/author-audio.py (also CC0).
